My name is Heinrich Georg Trückwitz. I was born in 1944 and lived most of my life in Stuttgart. Since my youth I have been engaged in genealogy and family history. Over many decades I have examined the life paths, kinship relations and historical contexts of numerous families.

Since the turmoil of the Covid pandemic I have lived entirely in the United States. Partly owing to my age, I no longer pursue extensive new research projects. Through this website, however, I would like to continue to add topics I have worked on, as well as research suggestions and comments on genealogy and heraldry, which are intended to encourage independent research and promote scholarly exchange.

The focus of my work lies particularly in the territories of the former Holy Roman Empire of the German Nation. My interest was not only in the well-known and frequently researched lineages, but also in families whose history is hardly known today and whose traces in the sources are becoming ever harder to find, as well as questions of noble and status law.
