Research Focus Areas

My interest lies both in the well-known and frequently studied lineages and in the lesser-known families of the German cultural sphere that are nevertheless significant for local and regional history. Alongside the great houses, the focus also includes bourgeois families, merchant and patrician lineages, and families of officials, scholars and officers. For a long time they held a formative position in their city or region, but their genealogical record is today often inadequately explored.

In addition there are legal topics of the Holy Roman Empire of the German Nation, insofar as they matter for the history of families, status and property.

My area of study is the former Holy Roman Empire of the German Nation, with a focus on southern Germany, as well as the formerly German-settled territories of Central and Eastern Europe, namely Breslau (Wrocław), Prague, Brünn (Brno), Danzig (Gdańsk), Königsberg (Kaliningrad), Stettin (Szczecin), Posen (Poznań), Reval (Tallinn) and Riga, as well as the Hanseatic cities of Lübeck, Hamburg and Bremen.

There the research draws on parish registers, citizen and council books, wills, and feudal and court records from state, city and noble archives. From these sources family connections, property relations and professional careers can be documented across several generations.
